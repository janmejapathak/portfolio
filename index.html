Who is Janmejay?

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student at Shri Ramswaroop Memorial University (SRMU), working at the intersection of full-stack engineering, data and applied AI.

My work sits in three places: building interfaces and APIs that actually ship, digging into data to find what it's trying to say, and wiring machine learning into products people can use. I like problems that don't have a tutorial.

I care about fundamentals — data structures, algorithms, clean architecture — because they're what make the flashy stuff possible. Currently going deeper into Data Science, AI/ML and cloud, one build at a time.
